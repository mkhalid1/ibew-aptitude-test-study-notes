# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when your test date is close. Both start with the [15-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know your weak areas. Expect about 45 to 90 minutes a day on the 7-day plan. Do all of it without a calculator.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your two weakest areas. |
| 2 | Formulas | Read [formulas and substitution](sections/formulas-and-substitution.md). Do all 5 items. For every miss, write one line: what you did, what the right step was. |
| 3 | Algebra | Read [expressions and equations](sections/expressions-and-equations.md). Do all 5 items, then 10 more solve and factor items from a textbook or free source. |
| 4 | Patterns and functions | Read [number patterns](sections/number-patterns.md) and [functions, tables and inequalities](sections/functions-tables-and-inequalities.md). Do both item sets. |
| 5 | Reading, then a timed run | Read [reading comprehension](sections/reading-comprehension.md) and do its 4 items. Then take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/njatc-aptitude-test?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ibew-aptitude-test). Read the [timing guide](timing.md). |
| 6 | Fix the weakest area | Reread the traps list for your weakest area. Redo its items without looking at the answers. Find at least 20 fresh items of that type. |
| 7 | Second timed run and rest | Take a second full timed test in the morning. Compare with day 5. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop studying by early evening. |

### How to review a missed item

The review is where the improvement comes from. For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **What went wrong?** For example: dropped a negative sign, forgot to flip an inequality, chose a true-sounding answer the passage did not support.
2. **What should have stopped me?** Name the step or the line in the passage.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "brackets around every negative I substitute."

Keep these in one list. By day 6 you will see the same two or three mistakes repeating, and fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when your test date is close. If you have more warning than this, use the 7-day plan: algebra rewards steady practice more than cramming.

### Day 1: learn the item types

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 25 min | Read the "How the questions look" and "Traps" parts of all five practice pages. |
| 3 | 45 min | Do the practice items for your two weakest areas. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right. |
| 4 | 50 min | Take one full timed test of the area you are least sure about, Algebra and Functions or Reading Comprehension. |
| 5 | 20 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: the day before the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 min | Redo the items you missed yesterday, without the answers. |
| 2 | 10 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 3 | 20 min | Ten mixed algebra items and one reading passage, at a relaxed pace. Stop if you feel tired. |
| 4 | Evening | Lay out your photo ID. Check the address and start time. Sleep. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 3, read the checklist, and go to bed.

## What not to do

- **Do not practise with a calculator.** You cannot use one on the day, and the habit is hard to drop quickly.
- **Do not hunt for "real" questions.** Learn the item types and the methods. That is what transfers.
- **Do not skip reading.** Reading Comprehension is 36 of the 69 items.
- **Do not cram the night before.** The battery takes about two and a half hours, and you need your focus at the end as much as at the start.

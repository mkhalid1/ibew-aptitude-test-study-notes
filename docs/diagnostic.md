# 15-minute diagnostic

Fifteen original items, three from each area of the battery, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 15 minutes, use paper and a pencil but no calculator, answer everything, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from the NJATC aptitude test or any publisher's materials.

If you already know the question types, skip this and take the [free full-length NJATC aptitude practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/njatc-aptitude-test?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ibew-aptitude-test) instead. A full timed run gives a better picture of pacing.

## Part A: Formulas and substitution

**D1.** Consider the formula L = 4w − 3. If w = 2.5, what is L?

A. 7 · B. 10 · C. 13 · D. 5.5

**D2.** Consider the formula M = (a + b) ÷ c. If a = 9, b = 15 and c = 4, what is M?

A. 12.75 · B. 9 · C. 3 · D. 6

**D3.** The perimeter of a rectangle is P = 2L + 2W. Which formula gives W by itself?

A. W = P − 2L · B. W = (P − 2L) ÷ 2 · C. W = P ÷ 2 − 2L · D. W = 2(P − L)

## Part B: Expressions and equations

**D4.** If 7x + 3 = 4x + 18, what is x?

A. 3 · B. 5 · C. 7 · D. 15

**D5.** Which expression is equal to (x + 6)(x − 2)?

A. x² + 4x − 12 · B. x² − 4x − 12 · C. x² + 8x − 12 · D. x² + 4x + 12

**D6.** Which is a factored form of x² + 2x − 15?

A. (x − 5)(x + 3) · B. (x + 15)(x − 1) · C. (x + 5)(x − 3) · D. (x − 5)(x − 3)

## Part C: Number patterns

**D7.** What is the next number? 4, 12, 36, 108, ...

A. 216 · B. 144 · C. 324 · D. 432

**D8.** What is the next number? 30, 28, 24, 18, ...

A. 12 · B. 10 · C. 8 · D. 14

**D9.** What is the next number? 2, 9, 4, 8, 6, 7, ...

A. 6 · B. 5 · C. 10 · D. 8

## Part D: Functions, tables and inequalities

**D10.** If f(x) = x² − 2x, what is f(−3)?

A. 3 · B. −3 · C. 15 · D. −15

**D11.** Which rule gives the values in this table?

| x | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 |

A. y = x + 2 · B. y = 2x − 1 · C. y = 2x + 1 · D. y = 3x − 2

**D12.** Which values of x make 5 − x ≥ 8 true?

A. x ≥ −3 · B. x ≥ 3 · C. x ≤ 3 · D. x ≤ −3

## Part E: Reading comprehension

> The Halvorsen Bridge, completed in 1931, was the first steel bridge across the Tanner River. Before it opened, wagons and later trucks crossed on a ferry that could carry only two vehicles at a time and did not run when the river flooded. The bridge was built with a central section that could be raised to let tall boats pass. By the 1970s river traffic had almost stopped, and the lifting machinery was sealed in place in 1978. Today the bridge carries about 9,000 vehicles a day, and engineers have recommended replacing its deck within the next ten years.

The bridge and river are fictional.

**D13.** According to the passage, what limited river crossings before 1931?

A. Tolls charged at the crossing · B. The raised central section · C. A ferry that carried two vehicles at a time and did not run in floods · D. A shortage of trucks

**D14.** What is the most likely reason the lifting machinery was sealed?

A. River traffic had almost stopped · B. The bridge was about to be replaced · C. A flood damaged the machinery · D. The bridge carried too many vehicles

**D15.** Which statement is best supported by the passage?

A. The bridge's deck has already been replaced · B. The central section can no longer be raised for boats · C. The ferry still runs during floods · D. The bridge carries about 9,000 vehicles a year

## What your result means

<summary>Answer key and explanations</summary>

| Item | D1 | D2 | D3 | D4 | D5 | D6 | D7 | D8 | D9 | D10 | D11 | D12 | D13 | D14 | D15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Answer | A | D | B | B | A | C | C | B | D | C | B | D | C | A | B |

- **D1: A (7).** 4 × 2.5 = 10, then 10 − 3 = 7.
- **D2: D (6).** The whole top is divided: (9 + 15) ÷ 4 = 24 ÷ 4 = 6. A, 12.75, divides only the 15.
- **D3: B.** Subtract 2L from both sides: P − 2L = 2W. Divide by 2. A forgets to divide.
- **D4: B (5).** 3x + 3 = 18, so 3x = 15 and x = 5.
- **D5: A.** x² − 2x + 6x − 12 = x² + 4x − 12.
- **D6: C.** You need two numbers that multiply to −15 and add to +2: +5 and −3.
- **D7: C (324).** Each term is multiplied by 3. 108 × 3 = 324.
- **D8: B (10).** Differences are −2, −4, −6, so the next is −8. 18 − 8 = 10.
- **D9: D (8).** Odd positions: 2, 4, 6. Even positions: 9, 8, 7. The 7th number is an odd position, so 8.
- **D10: C (15).** (−3)² − 2(−3) = 9 + 6 = 15.
- **D11: B.** y rises by 2 for each 1 in x, and 2 × 1 − 1 = 1. Check: 2 × 4 − 1 = 7.
- **D12: D (x ≤ −3).** Subtract 5: −x ≥ 3. Multiply by −1 and flip: x ≤ −3.
- **D13: C.** The ferry "could carry only two vehicles at a time and did not run when the river flooded."
- **D14: A.** The passage puts the two facts side by side: river traffic had almost stopped, and the machinery was sealed. Nothing mentions damage or replacement.
- **D15: B.** The machinery was "sealed in place," so the section can no longer be lifted. D confuses a day with a year, and A confuses a recommendation with something that has happened.

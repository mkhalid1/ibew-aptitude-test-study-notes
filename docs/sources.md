# Sources

Every fact in these notes about the test comes from the test's developer or from a local program's own published page. Each source below was checked live on 24 November 2026. If something has changed, please [open an issue](https://github.com/mkhalid1/ibew-aptitude-test-study-notes/issues).

## Test developer: electrical training ALLIANCE

| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| [Applying for Apprenticeship](https://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/SamplePage/ApplyingforApprenticeship) | Basic requirements, the two tests and their items and minutes, about two and a half hours, the break, results in two to four weeks, the retake rule, no guessing penalty, exact scores not provided, no calculator, photo ID, materials provided, about 3 hours at the center, arriving 15 minutes early, incomplete tests, the interview, ranking and two-year eligibility list, accommodations |
| [Preparing for the Test](https://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/SamplePage/PreparingfortheTest) | What each test measures, the kinds of items in the official samples, one correct answer per item, the sample passage being shorter than real ones, applicant numbers relative to openings |
| [About Us](https://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/AboutUs) | The ALLIANCE was previously called the NJATC and is a joint program of NECA and the IBEW |

We describe the official sample items only by type. None of their wording, numbers or passages appear in these notes.

## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not official statements, and are labelled as guidance where they appear:

- the per-item timing budgets and clock checkpoints
- the "about 80% on timed practice" target
- the step-by-step methods, traps lists and study plans
- the split of Algebra and Functions into four practice pages
- all practice items, passages and explanations, which are original

## Not affiliated

These notes are independent. They are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the IBEW, NECA, the electrical training ALLIANCE or any JATC.

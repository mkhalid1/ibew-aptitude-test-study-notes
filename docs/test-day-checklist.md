# Test-day checklist

The NJATC aptitude test battery is taken at a test center your local JATC arranges. The electrical training ALLIANCE's reminders are short and strict, so start with those.

## The day before

- [ ] Check the date, time and address on your test notice.
- [ ] Put your **photo ID** where you cannot forget it. The ALLIANCE's rule: "No ID, No Test, No Exceptions!"
- [ ] Arrange childcare. Children are not allowed at the test center.
- [ ] Plan on about **3 hours** at the center, and arrange work or transport around that.
- [ ] If you asked for an accommodation, confirm it has been arranged. Requests made on the day cannot be handled that day.
- [ ] Do a short warm-up only: a few algebra items and one reading passage. No full practice test.
- [ ] Get a good night's sleep.

## The morning of the test

- [ ] Eat a proper meal. The battery runs for about two and a half hours.
- [ ] Leave your calculator at home. If your watch has a built-in calculator, you will be asked to remove it, so wear a plain watch or none.
- [ ] Aim to arrive **at least 15 minutes early**.

You do not need to bring pencils or paper. The ALLIANCE says pencils and all other materials are provided.

## During the test

- [ ] Listen to the instructions in full, even if you have read about the test.
- [ ] Use your scratch paper for every calculation. Write the brackets.
- [ ] Check the clock at your pacing checkpoints. See the [timing guide](timing.md).
- [ ] Skip and mark any item that passes two minutes. Come back if you have time.
- [ ] Answer every item. There is no penalty for guessing.
- [ ] Use the break between the tests to reset: stand up, drink water, forget the algebra.
- [ ] In reading, find the line for every answer before you choose it.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Write down your test date. It sets the earliest date you could retest, if you need to.
- [ ] Expect results to reach your JATC in about two to four weeks.
- [ ] If you qualify, start preparing for the interview with the NECA and IBEW committee.
- [ ] If you do not, ask your JATC when you may retest. Do not book before the wait is over: a retest taken too early is not valid. See [retakes](scoring.md#retakes).

## Last practice before the real one

A day or two before the test, do one relaxed timed run so the pace feels familiar, such as the [free NJATC aptitude practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/njatc-aptitude-test?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ibew-aptitude-test). Then stop and rest.

# IBEW Aptitude Test Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for the IBEW aptitude test, officially the NJATC aptitude test battery used by IBEW and NECA electrical apprenticeship programs. It covers how the battery works, a clear method for every item type in Algebra and Functions and in Reading Comprehension, 34 original practice items with full explanations, what is and is not published about scoring, and study plans for a week or a weekend. All of it is written to be done the way the real test is taken: by hand, without a calculator. Facts about the test come only from the electrical training ALLIANCE and local programs' own pages, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed NJATC aptitude practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/njatc-aptitude-test?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=tv2v2s&utm_content=ibew-aptitude-test), or the shorter [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Find your weak areas.** Take the [15-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md). It has two items from each area and tells you where to start.
2. **Fix the weakest area first.** Open its guide below, learn the method, then do its 5 practice items.
3. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your test date is close.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Official name | NJATC aptitude test battery |
| Developed by | electrical training ALLIANCE (previously called NJATC) |
| Used by | IBEW and NECA electrical apprenticeship programs, through local JATCs |
| Algebra and Functions | 33 items, 46 minutes |
| Reading Comprehension | 36 items, 51 minutes |
| Total | About two and a half hours, with a short break. Plan on about 3 hours at the center. |
| Calculator | Not allowed |
| Guessing | No penalty. Score is based on the number correct. |
| Results | To your JATC in about two to four weeks. You are told whether you qualified, not your exact score. |
| Qualifying score | Set by each local program. Not published nationally. [Examples](docs/scoring.md#the-1-to-9-scale-and-qualifying-scores) |
| Retakes | Not before three months (90 days) from your most recent test. [Full rule](docs/scoring.md#retakes) |
| Electrical knowledge | Not required |

Source: [electrical training ALLIANCE, Applying for Apprenticeship](https://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/SamplePage/ApplyingforApprenticeship), checked 24 November 2026.

## The item types at a glance

| Area | Test | What you do | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formulas and substitution | Algebra and Functions | Put values into a formula, or rearrange it | [Formulas](docs/sections/formulas-and-substitution.md) |
| Expressions and equations | Algebra and Functions | Solve, expand, factor and simplify | [Equations](docs/sections/expressions-and-equations.md) |
| Number patterns | Algebra and Functions | Find the next, missing or far term | [Patterns](docs/sections/number-patterns.md) |
| Functions, tables and inequalities | Algebra and Functions | Match rules to tables, solve inequalities, read lines | [Functions](docs/sections/functions-tables-and-inequalities.md) |
| Reading comprehension | Reading Comprehension | Answer detail, main idea, inference and vocabulary questions on passages | [Reading](docs/sections/reading-comprehension.md) |

The real battery has two timed tests. These notes split Algebra and Functions into four guides so you can practise one skill at a time. Every guide has the same parts: what it measures, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice, 5 original practice items with explanations (4 for reading comprehension), and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Work without a calculator.** That is how the real test is taken. Use paper and a pencil.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations." Commit to an answer before you open them.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Most say why the most tempting wrong answer is wrong, which is where the learning is.
- **Keep a miss log.** For each miss, write what went wrong. The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** The notes teach the methods. A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/njatc-aptitude-test?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=tv2v2s&utm_content=ibew-aptitude-test), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want your own annotated copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

All 34 practice items are original, written for these notes. None are taken from the NJATC aptitude test, the electrical training ALLIANCE's materials or any prep site. They follow the item types shown in the official samples so that the methods you learn here transfer to the real test.
